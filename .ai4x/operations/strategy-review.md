# Scope

Load for strategy design, terminology review, source-grounded instance
analysis, or an independent review by an external strategist.

# Strategy Standard

- Separate source statements, authors' derivations, fachlich authorized
  decisions, and unknowns.
- Never invent missing Ethos, Mission, Vision, Strategy, Need, relation, or
  evidence content.
- Evaluate strategic logic, not only O2I conformance: diagnosis, intent,
  guiding policy, positioning, trade-offs, coherent action commitments,
  strategic success reference, and Fit must form a defensible whole where
  applicable.
- Macrorelations require meaningful Primitive evidence. Missing evidence may
  remain an explicit Candidate or unknown; it must never be silently completed.
- Treat concrete-instance friction as possible evidence of a generic O2I
  design issue. Improve O2I only when the generalized design is independently
  justified.

# Independent Strategy Review

Apply `../TEAM.md` for independence and `../governance/guidelines.md` for review
evidence, remedies, and verdicts. Assess every materially affected dimension:

- fachliche soundness and strategic coherence;
- source grounding and explicit authors' derivations;
- terminology completeness and correct semantic placement;
- whether Terminology remains independently fachlich understandable and every
  metamodel or specification refinement stays at its proper abstraction level;
- Need qualification, measurement, effect, evidence, target attainment,
  traceability, and plausible attribution;
- practical applicability, limitations, and governance implications;
- whether O2I creates genuine strategic value without unnecessary ceremony.

Report blocking findings before advisory follow-ups; use severity only to
prioritize correction. Include the overall strategic value in the verdict.
